Our privacy principles

Consent-led access
The older adult chooses who joins their circle.
Minimum necessary data
Share coordination details, not more personal information than needed.
Clear roles
Family, care providers and coordinators receive different access.
Visible activity
Members can review important updates and changes.
Prototype disclaimer
This is a front-end concept with sample data. It does not send alerts, contact providers, store data securely on a server, verify identities, or provide emergency monitoring. A production service needs authentication, encrypted storage, consent records, role-based access, audit logs, secure notifications and a tested emergency escalation process.
